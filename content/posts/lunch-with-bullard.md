![David Bullard](/content/images/2007/08/davidbullard.jpg)[David Bullard](http://blogs.thetimes.co.za/bullard/?ref=justhart.com), _acclaimed_ journalist and public enemy number one, recently invited me to an extended lunch with some other elite bloggers – [youngBLOOD](http://blog.colindaniels.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) and [Groogle](http://www.groogle.co.za/?ref=justhart.com). It was inevitable really that our paths would cross and I have to say I feel most privileged that I have had the opportunity to meet the man in person.

After calling David a [wanker](/2007/05/07/is-david-bullard-an-utter-wanker/) one would imagine the man would never want to talk to me again however he has been a true gentleman about the [Bullardgate](http://vincentmaher.com/mit/?p=347&ref=justhart.com) affair and in particular my reaction to the whole event.

I have to admit that I really enjoyed my lunch at [The Grill House](http://www.thegrillhouse.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) with Bullard and I noticed this morning that he [sneakily took a snap](http://blogs.thetimes.co.za/bullard/?p=106&ref=justhart.com) of me on his new Nokia phone which he’s now trying to sell on eBay.

David, you do realise this picture of me is better suited for [Sotheby’s](http://www.sothebys.com/?ref=justhart.com)? I am after-all a highly sought after individual.
