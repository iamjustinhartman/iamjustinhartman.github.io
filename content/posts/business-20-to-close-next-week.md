When someone told me last night that [Business 2.0](http://money.cnn.com/magazines/business2/?ref=justhart.com) is closing their doors I simply couldn’t believe it. After being [featured](/2007/07/30/it-is-a-web-20-world-and-were-part-of-it/) in the magazine a month ago it came as a big shock to the system but the New York Times blog has the [full story](http://bits.blogs.nytimes.com/2007/09/04/business-20-rip/?ref=justhart.com). I’m just not sure how you can make such a loss with 623,000 subscribers in your database but I guess this case just shows that traditional media has to evolve – or die.
