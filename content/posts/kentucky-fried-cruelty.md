Over the weekend I learned about how [KFC](http://www.kfc.com/?ref=justhart.com) suppliers overpopulate birds in their factories, drug them and boil them alive to remove their feathers. I honestly couldn’t believe what I was hearing so I did some further investigation.

It turns out that [PETA](http://www.peta.org/about/index.asp?ref=justhart.com), an animal rights organisation, along with PETA activist [Pamela Anderson](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_anderson?ref=justhart.com) have formed the [Kentucky Fried Cruelty](http://www.kentuckyfriedcruelty.com/?ref=justhart.com) campaign to help spread the word.
