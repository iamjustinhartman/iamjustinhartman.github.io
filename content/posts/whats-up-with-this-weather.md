![Johannesburg Temperatures](/content/images/2007/09/jhb-temp.png)

Looking at my [Dashboard Widget](http://www.apple.com/downloads/dashboard/?ref=justhart.com) at around 00:00 I noticed that today’s high was 32 degrees and that the current temperature is 23 degrees. Now forgive me if I am wrong but isn’t this still supposed to be Spring in Johannesburg? This is not the first time either that we’ve hit well above the 30 degree mark and I have to say I’m dreading December if this is a prelude of things to come.

Makes you wonder if maybe we shouldn’t start taking a serious look at what we’re doing to our climate through the effects of [global warming](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_warming?ref=justhart.com).
