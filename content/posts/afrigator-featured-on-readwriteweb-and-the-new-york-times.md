[![](/content/images/2008/12/150-red-star.jpg "150-red-star")](http://www.readwriteweb.com/archives/top_10_international_products_2008.php?ref=justhart.com)This almost went unnoticed but on Friday [Afrigator](http://afrigator.com/?ref=justhart.com) was voted one of the **Top 10 International Products for 2008** by [ReadWriteWeb](http://www.readwriteweb.com/archives/top_10_international_products_2008.php?ref=justhart.com). This is obviously a massive accolade for all of us involved in Afrigator and at times like this I’m left perplexed at how a small _garage startup_ like Afrigator has managed to gain such credibility outside the borders of SA.

To be included in the list along with massive properties like [Netvibes](http://www.netvibes.com/?ref=justhart.com) and [Zoho](http://www.zoho.com/?ref=justhart.com) is a great testament to the efforts the guys have put into this company and I was particularly chuffed to see that this article made the [New York Times’ Technology](http://www.nytimes.com/external/readwriteweb/2008/12/05/05readwriteweb-top_10_international_products_2008.html?ref=justhart.com) section.

[![Afrigator on the New York Times](http://farm4.static.flickr.com/3142/3090489934_2b5f13831d.jpg)](http://www.nytimes.com/external/readwriteweb/2008/12/05/05readwriteweb-top_10_international_products_2008.html?ref=justhart.com)

Now all we need to do is figure out how we can get similar local recognition and then we’re well and truly sorted!
