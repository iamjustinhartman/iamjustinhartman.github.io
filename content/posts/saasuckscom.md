What do you get when you…

• …install a brand new WordPress instance on a fancy [new domain name](http://saasucks.com/?ref=justhart.com)?

• …steal [FlySAA](http://www.flysaa.com/?ref=justhart.com)‘s home page banner and insert it into the WordPress default theme?

• …do a [Google search](http://www.google.co.za/search?q=%22saa+sucks%22&ie=utf-8&oe=utf-8&aq=t&rls=org.mozilla:en-US:official&client=firefox-a&ref=justhart.com) for other people who’ve complained about SAA?

The answer… [SAA Sucks](http://saasucks.com/?ref=justhart.com).

[![](/content/images/2008/10/picture-5-500x228.png "South African Airlines Sucks")](http://saasucks.com/?ref=justhart.com)

Seriously though, the whole [getting ripped off experience](/2008/10/21/saa-extortion-at-its-best/) I’ve recently gone through with [South African Airways](http://saasucks.com/?ref=justhart.com) has really hit me hard. What’s more is that I’ve realised that not only do most people feel the same way but many have gone through similar experiences.

Added to this, my letter of demand to Khaya Ngqula (CEO of [SAA](http://saasucks.com/?ref=justhart.com)) seems to have fallen on deaf ears and I’ve been left with a bad taste in my mouth ever since.

Thanks to [Gregor](http://gregorrohrig.com/?ref=justhart.com) for giving me the idea to do a site around this and in a short space of time I managed to get SAA Sucks up and running. The idea behind it is to dedicate space on the interwebs for anyone to go and complain about [SAA](http://saasucks.com/?ref=justhart.com).

It is clear that [SAA](http://saasucks.com/?ref=justhart.com) are living in complete arrogance and have a total disregard for their clients and I think we need to take a stand. I’ve been a big pundit of the power of social media and now we can really put it to the test and see if we can make any difference whatsoever.

So while SAA Sucks is now live it’s up to you guys to help me spread the word. Write a blog post about it, send the link to your Facebook friends, post it on Twitter, Google bomb SAA with a link to SAA Sucks, frankly I don’t care – just spread the word.

Finally, If there are [any](http://www.robinpietersen.com/?ref=justhart.com) [designers](http://www.markforrester.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) [out](http://foxinni.com/?ref=justhart.com) [there](http://coda.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) who’d like to do a _proper theme_ for this site let me know – it could do with something decent!
