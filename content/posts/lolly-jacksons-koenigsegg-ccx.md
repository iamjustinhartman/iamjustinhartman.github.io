Lolly Jackson, owner of Teazers, has just purchased himself a brand new [Koenigsegg CCX](http://www.koenigsegg.com/cars_1_1.html?ref=justhart.com) super-car at a whopping R14 million. This makes the Koenigsegg almost double the price of a Ferrari Enzo…

South Africa will receive 3 of the 30 cars made a year and I wonder who the other two cars are going to. Personally I don’t like the Koenigsegg and I’d much rather own an [Audi R8](http://groogle.co.za/2008/03/26/out-to-lunch-with-david-bullard-and-the-audi-r8/?ref=justhart.com) (or 10 at the price of the Koenigsegg) but it’s something to behold nonetheless.
