[Warwick Poole](http://blog.linuxinternet.org/?ref=justhart.com), a born and bred South African now living in New York, is the Systemologist for [Automattic](http://automattic.com/?ref=justhart.com) – the company that runs [WordPress](http://wordpress.org/?ref=justhart.com).

I’m not sure what a Systemologist does but Warwick has just showcased The Times’ new [Multimedia Portal](http://multimedia.thetimes.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) on one of the Automattic blogs and you can read the [full post here](http://publisherblog.automattic.com/2008/05/28/the-times-multimedia-portal-runs-on-wordpress/?ref=justhart.com).
