[Gregor](http://groogle.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) and I have just finished delivering a presentation to [RedCube](http://www.redcube.co.za/?ref=justhart.com), the PR agency behind Visa, on Multimedia and Social Media. Gregor took the first part of the presentation where he highlights what [The Times](http://www.thetimes.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) is doing on a Multimedia level and also analyzes current global and local trends within in the Multimedia industry.

I took an in depth look at Social Media, Web 2.0 and the impact this has on PR agencies globally. There is an interesting case study I did on the [Frito-Lay](http://www.fritolay.com/?ref=justhart.com) PR department and how they utilised the Social Media sphere to promote the [Doritos](http://www.fritolay.com/fl/flstore/cgi-bin/products_doritos.htm?ref=justhart.com) chips brand among their core target market of 16-24 year olds. I also tried to look at what South African PR companies can do to further promote their clients’ brands in the Social Media sphere.
