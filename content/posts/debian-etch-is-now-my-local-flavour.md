After grabbling long and hard about the whole [Ubuntu dropping PowerPC support](/2006/12/05/power-pc-canned-by-ubuntu/) issue I have made a decision to move my Apple iBook G4 from my [recently installed](/2006/11/20/ibook-g4-now-sporting-ubuntu-edgy-eft/) Ubuntu 6.10 to the ever popular [Debian](http://www.debian.com/?ref=justhart.com) platform.

[Mark Shuttleworth’s email](/2006/12/07/mark-shuttleworth-puts-me-in-my-place/) to me and the whole debate that has gone on about Ubuntu’s decision to drop PowerPC has made this decision difficult however seeing as I have only recently moved from Apple OSX to Ubuntu I figured it’s better to move now and stick with a distro that will support PowerPC for probably a few more years than be stuck with a distro that is left to the devices of a community that might simply let PowerPC support die a slow and bitter death.  
  
The overall consensus I got from the [Ubuntu Forums](http://www.ubuntuforums.org/?ref=justhart.com) is that PowerPC users are already looking for a new Linux distro which has also played a big part in deciding to move to Debian. That said I will still support Ubuntu and keep my current 6.10 installations on my PC at home and work while my iBook will now begin the big migrate to Debian.

I have also recently offered my services to the [Ubuntu Za LoCo Team](http://www.ubuntu-za.org/?ref=justhart.com) and I will be doing work with other South African Ubuntu users to help promote and get the Ubuntu name out in the local community.

So, here’s to a new installation and hopefully the last for quite a while!
