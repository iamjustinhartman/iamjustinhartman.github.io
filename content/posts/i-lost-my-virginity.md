Well it’s official – I’m no longer a virgin and my first introduction into the _real-world_ geek community was fab. At first glance the [27dinner](http://27dinner.pbwiki.com/?ref=justhart.com) can be a little overwhelming however when you actually start introducing yourself to people it’s amazing to see how many people you actually know – even if just virtually.

Lots has been written about this unique 27dinner thing and I unfortunately don’t have anything unique to add to the discussion apart from the fact that I enjoyed myself immensely. I never really thought meeting this virtual lot could actually be such fun!
