Yesterday the family and I shipped off to the V&A Waterfront to pay a visit to the [Two Oceans Aquarium](http://www.aquarium.co.za/?ref=justhart.com). The aquarium in itself was awesome but after the expedition I noticed a yacht in the harbor which I simply had to go and see.

It turns out the magnificent yacht docked in Cape Town’s harbor is none other than [Paul Allen’s](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Allen?ref=justhart.com) _smaller_ yacht called the [Tatoosh](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatoosh_\(yacht\)?ref=justhart.com).

[![Paul Allen's yacht - Tatoosh](http://farm4.static.flickr.com/3275/2956111368_9b98b9e8b5.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/justinhartman/2956111368/?ref=justhart.com "Paul Allen's yacht - Tatoosh by Justin Hartman, on Flickr")

For those who don’t know the Microsoft co-founder’s yacht has been here since October 15th and had I not seen the two helicopters parked on the deck (see pic below) then we might have missed this fantastic sight.

Paul Allen paid no less than $100 million for the 92m long Tatoosh and it features:-

-   Five decks
-   a master suite, a saloon and other rooms on the top deck
-   a saloon with a French limestone fireplace, a dining area, staterooms and a ladies’ powder room on the main deck
-   a lobster tank
-   a shaded 1.8m deep swimming pool
-   a movie theater
-   facilities to transport two helicopters on the top two decks (see pic below)
-   a custom Hinckley powerboat about 12m
-   a Hinckley sailboat of about 12m

[![Paul Allen's yacht - Tatoosh](http://farm4.static.flickr.com/3030/2955274415_e6d7e63562.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/justinhartman/2955274415/?ref=justhart.com "Paul Allen's yacht - Tatoosh by Justin Hartman, on Flickr")

Paul also owns the [Octopus](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopus_\(yacht\)?ref=justhart.com) which is the larger of his two yachts at 126m long. This makes the Octopus the second largest super yacht that is not owned by a head of state.
