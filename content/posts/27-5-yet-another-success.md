One of the first things [Nic Haralambous](http://nicharalambous.com/?ref=justhart.com) had to say to me on arrival at the [27dinner](http://27dinner.pbwiki.com/?ref=justhart.com) was – _why are you not at home working?_ My response was quite simply that once every two months I take a night off to socialise and interact with like-minded Geeks and last night was no different.

It’s only my second 27dinner but I have to admit that I really look forward to these Geek gatherings as it gives me an opportunity to chat with all these virtual users in a real-world kind of way. I had some awesome chats with [Paul de Sousa](http://blogs.thetimes.co.za/desousa?ref=justhart.com), [Vinny Lingham](http://www.vinnylingham.com/?ref=justhart.com) (who despite having such a HUGE reputation is actually such a down-to-earth guy), [Vincent Maher](http://vincentmaher.com/?ref=justhart.com), [Colin Daniels](http://blog.colindaniels.co.za/?ref=justhart.com), [Susan Deysel](http://how-to-become-a-serial-dater.blogspot.com/?ref=justhart.com) (who couldn’t remember me for love or money) and the infamous Erik Hersman who is more commonly know as the [White African](http://www.whiteafrican.com/?ref=justhart.com).

Colin gave us an interesting debriefing on [Bullardgate](http://www.facebook.com/group.php?gid=2445995529) while Erik really drilled home mobile phone usage as a medium to deliver content to users and finally Vinny made his [big announcement](http://www.vinnylingham.com/2007/05/the-road-ahead-for-me.html?ref=justhart.com).

Last night also gave us an opportunity to hand out the [Afrigator](http://afrigator.com/?ref=justhart.com) [Limited Edition T-Shirts](http://blog.afrigator.com/2007/04/26/win-an-apple-ipod-with-afrigator/?ref=justhart.com) so I really hope everyone who got one is happy with them!

Once again thanks to [Dave](http://daveduarte.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) and [Mike](http://mikestopforth.com/?ref=justhart.com) from [Cerebra](http://www.cerebra.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) for organising, paying and hosting the event and a special thanks to Mike for ~eventually~ remembering to give me a lift home!
