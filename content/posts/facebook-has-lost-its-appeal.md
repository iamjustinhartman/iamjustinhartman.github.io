[![facebook applications](http://farm2.static.flickr.com/1215/547367736_43fd411aa4_o.png)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/justinhartman/547367736/?ref=justhart.com "Photo Sharing")When I heard about the launch of [Facebook Apps](http://developers.facebook.com/anatomy.php?ref=justhart.com) I got a little concerned and I think, rightfully so. Facebook is the [Apple](http://www.apple.com/?ref=justhart.com) of Social Networking sites and it’s clean and fresh approach has held the key to its success. I see evidence of this simplicity when friends of mine, who don’t even own computers, have a Facebook profile.

Facebook Apps however is changing the very focus of this simplicity and I’ve noticed a huge influx of requests for me to add various third-party applications to [my profile](http://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=566420412). It is clear that Zuckerman and his company have probably more than doubled their market value by allowing companies to integrate with Facebook but I think from an end-user’s perspective it’s a bad move.

Since these horrible requests started streaming in (see pic) I’ve found myself using Facebook less and less and while I’m not a conventional user of Facebook I feel it’s lost its value for me.

I think that like [Twitter](http://twitter.com/?ref=justhart.com), Facebook will become a thing of past for me.
