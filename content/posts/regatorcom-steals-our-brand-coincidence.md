I’m not sure how I feel about this.

![](/content/images/2008/07/picture-7.png "Regator steals Afrigator brand?")

Regator.com went into private beta today and I picked it up via this [Techcrunch article](http://www.techcrunch.com/2008/07/03/regator-wants-to-be-a-blog-reader-for-the-masses/?ref=justhart.com). They’ve also got great exposure on [ReadWriteWeb](http://www.readwriteweb.com/cgi-bin/mt/mt-tb.cgi/4353?ref=justhart.com) and even [Mashable](http://mashable.com/2008/07/03/regator/?ref=justhart.com).

[These guys](http://www.regator.com/blog/?p=21&ref=justhart.com) are a blog aggregator that not only use the [Afrigator](http://afrigator.com/?ref=justhart.com) crocodile, similar domain name and colour scheme and I have to feel this is a little more than a coincidence.

There is little doubt that [Mike](http://www.mikestopforth.com/?ref=justhart.com), [Stii](http://stii.za.net/?ref=justhart.com), [Mark](http://www.markforrester.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) and myself will need to speak to our [legal team](http://jacobson.co.za/?ref=justhart.com) to find out what action we can take on this.

What do you guys think?

**UPDATE: I’ve had a long chat with Scott from Regator and I’m now convinced it was a coincidence. Please see my [updated post here](/2008/07/04/regatorcom-update/).**
